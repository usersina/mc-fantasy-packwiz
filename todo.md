# Build Recommendation for Vampiric Blood + Ice

**My Recommendation**
Play **Blood + Ice**.

Blood supplies damage, healing, summons, invisibility, and blood restoration. Ice supplies the missing crowd control, ranged pressure, and protection against enemies that resist Blood, especially undead. This matches the intended school identities in [Iron’s school guide](https://iron.wiki/1_19/schools/).

Your vampire compatibility addon makes Holy a particularly bad second school: Holy healing damages vampire casters, while several utility spells hurt you and fail. [Vampirism Iron’s Spells Compatibility](https://modrinth.com/mod/vampirism-irons-spells-compatibility)

**Endgame Armor**

| Slot  | Item                 |
| ----- | -------------------- |
| Head  | Cursium Mage Circlet |
| Chest | Cultist Armor        |
| Legs  | Cultist Leggings     |
| Feet  | Cursium Mage Greaves |

I checked these directly in your installed jars. Before upgrade orbs, that combination provides:

- `+550` maximum mana
- `+20%` generic spell power
- `+20%` Blood spell power
- `+40%` Ice spell power
- `+20%` Ice resistance
- 24 nominal armor and 8 armor toughness

The Circlet provides Ghost Vision, while the Greaves reduce fall damage and provide the backstep ability.

I specifically excluded the **Cursium Mage Breastplate**. Its independent Undead Revive mechanic could interfere with your carefully patched Vampirism and Revive Me death sequence.

Until you obtain Cursium equipment, use:

- Cultist chestplate and leggings
- Cryomancer hood and boots

Full Cultist remains the maximum pure-Blood setup, but the mixed set is considerably more versatile.

**Weapons**

Your primary casting weapon should be **The Blood Staff**:

- 8 melee damage
- `+15%` Blood spell power
- `+5%` generic spell power
- `+10%` summon damage

Your melee sidearm should be an **Ultimate Heartseeker** with:

- Vampire Bite III
- Sharpness
- Looting
- Unbreaking
- Mending

Keep the Heartseeker on your hotbar and switch to it for melee finishers. The Blood Staff should normally stay in your main hand while casting.

**Spellbook**

Continue using the **Vampiric Spell Book**, equipped in its Curios slot. It has ten slots and provides:

- `+200` mana
- `+10%` Blood spell power
- `+10%` spell resistance
- Affinity for Blood Slash and Blood Step

With the proposed armor and Blood Staff, your unupgraded totals become approximately:

- **Blood:** `+70%` effective spell power
- **Ice:** `+65%` effective spell power
- **Mana:** `+750`

Keep the **Necronomicon** as an alternate summoning book. Its six slots are restrictive, but its built-in level-five Blood spells and Raise Dead affinity make it excellent for focused combat. Iron’s documents its unique progression and fixed spells in the [official changelog](https://iron.wiki/1_19/changelog/).

**Ten-Spell Loadout**

1. Blood Step
2. Ray of Siphoning
3. Devour
4. Blood Slash
5. Raise Dead
6. Ray of Frost
7. Frostwave
8. Ice Tomb
9. Ice Spikes
10. Blizzard or Cone of Cold

Ray of Frost sets up freezing, Frostwave controls crowds around you, and Ice Tomb gives you a defensive reset. Blood Step remains your opener and escape tool.

**Curios**

- **Ring of the Lurker:** Blood Step gives true invisibility, and the ring makes your first invisible attack deal `+50%` damage.
- **Ring of Recovery:** dependable cooldown reduction.
- **Amulet of Concentration:** prevents Ray of Siphoning and Cone of Cold from being interrupted.
- **Red Iron Umbrella:** keep it in the dedicated umbrella slot.
- Replace the amulet with a **Greater Conjurer’s Talisman** when focusing on Raise Dead and other summons.

For armor enchantments, strongly consider Fire Protection IV on one piece because your configured vampires take triple fire damage. Use Protection IV on the remaining pieces.

This keeps you unmistakably a vampire Blood mage while giving you a genuinely useful second school rather than another flavor of teleportation or raw damage.
